Secure Online Voting System

A next-generation electronic voting architecture powered by David Chaum RSA Blind Signatures, SHA-256 Blockchain Hash Chaining, and enterprise Amazon Web Services (AWS) cloud infrastructure.

🔐 Cryptographic Privacy

Uses David Chaum's 2048-bit RSA Blind Signatures. The election authority verifies your eligibility and signs your ballot without ever seeing who you voted for.

⛓️ Tamper-Evident Ledger

Every ballot is anchored into an append-only SHA-256 hash chain linked back to Genesis. Any database alteration is instantly detected and alerts security engineers.

☁️ AWS Well-Architected

Deployed across Custom VPC, Amazon RDS MySQL in private subnets, EC2 with Gunicorn & systemd, Parameter Store KMS encryption, and S3 audit vaults.
